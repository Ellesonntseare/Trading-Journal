EDGE Trading Journal — Buffer Video + Post Formats Update
=========================================================

Changes
-------
- Facebook: choose Feed post, Story or Reel.
- Instagram: choose Feed post, Story or Reel; optional share-to-feed checkbox.
- YouTube: video title and category fields.
- Upload MP4/MOV videos up to 200 MB each, with 1 GB per-user library quota.
- Select a saved video, preview it, and schedule or queue it to multiple channels.
- Server validates per-channel media requirements and returns individual results.
- Selected channels receive the same timestamp in Schedule mode.
- Screenshots and videos are mutually exclusive per post.

There are NO new npm dependencies in this update.

Install over your EXISTING Phase 5/Buffer Scheduler journal
-----------------------------------------------------------
1. Back up the whole existing journal directory (especially data/ and images/).
2. Stop ONLY the EDGE process: pm2 stop edge
3. Copy these four files from this update into your existing journal directory:
      server.js
      public/buffer.js
      public/buffer.css
      public/index.html
   Preserve the public/ subdirectory. Overwrite the four existing files.
4. Restart: pm2 restart edge
5. Run: pm2 save
6. Reload http://localhost:3000/#/buffer (Ctrl+Shift+R).

Do NOT overwrite your existing data/, images/, config.json or .env files.
Do NOT delete your existing data or images.

How to publish
--------------
- To publish a screenshot, pick up to four saved screenshots in the gallery.
- To publish a video, choose MP4/MOV, click Upload video, then select the video.
- Select your connected social channels.
- If Facebook selected, choose Post, Story or Reel.
- If Instagram selected, choose Post, Story or Reel.
- If YouTube selected, upload a video and complete its title and category.
- Set Action > Schedule for a specific time, and choose date/time, or use Queue/Draft.
- Check the per-channel success/failure results. Never automatically retry
  successful channels, or you may create duplicate posts.

IMPORTANT: PUBLIC HTTPS MEDIA ADDRESS
-------------------------------------
Buffer does not accept local file uploads via its API. Your journal must have
an externally accessible HTTPS address for the unguessable image/video URLs.
Videos uploaded to the journal are PRIVATE until selected for publishing.
When you send a post, its specific media file is accessible through a long,
random, public URL, which Buffer needs to fetch (sometimes at publishing time).
Keep your server running, maintain the HTTPS address, and do not delete a
scheduled video's underlying file until after publication is complete.
Do NOT use localhost or LAN addresses for Buffer media.
Your chosen platform may have additional video length, aspect ratio, codec,
account, or permission restrictions. MP4/H.264/AAC is broadly compatible.

Note: No live publish was performed with your Buffer credentials.
